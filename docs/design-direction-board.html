01 — The direction

Three sources, three jobs

Build an established, calm, typography‑led site for skilled nursing — not a startup SaaS template, not a clinical healthcare site. The credibility comes from one verified number and disciplined hierarchy, not from stock photography we don't have time to source well.

Rather than blending reference sites by percentage, each source has a defined job:

Tone

PointClickCare

Calm, sector-specific, unhurried. Warm neutrals over cold corporate gray. Industry language, not generic HR-speak.

Discipline

Stripe

Hierarchy, spacing, modular composition, and the treatment of proof at scale. Execution only — none of the startup polish.

Identity

SBV itself

Burgundy, the clock mark, operational vocabulary, and 700 installed facilities. Neither reference supplies this.

02 — Palette

Keep the red. Deepen it. Warm the neutrals.

SBV's existing theme runs on #b5222e over cool gray — bright enough to read as an alert state, which is a liability in healthcare. Deepening toward burgundy fixes that while keeping real brand equity. Warm off‑white instead of cool gray is the highest-leverage move for warmth without photography.

03 — Typography

One sans. Hierarchy through weight and space, never color.

Production sets Inter. This board renders in the system sans, which is close but not identical — glyph shapes are indicative, the scale and weights are the proposal. Open Sans (the incumbent) is dropped: it reads as a 2016 WordPress theme, which is the exact impression we're shedding.

05 — Capabilities

Structured type, not feature cards

Three of six shown. Thin dividers, a short accent rule, one outcome sentence each. No screenshots, no invented UI, no platform diagram — the legacy interface misrepresents the quality we're claiming, and mocking up screens the product doesn't have is a promise it has to keep at demo time.

06 — The one dark section

Where the differentiation lands

Used exactly once on the homepage. Burgundy, never navy — navy would pull the whole site back toward generic B2B.

Built for long-term care

Staffing a facility is not the same as staffing an office.

Census-driven staffing levels, overnight shifts, multiple job classifications, direct-care reporting, credentials, and multi-facility operations all have to work together — and end in payroll-ready data. SBV is built around that, not adapted to it.

08 — Deliberately absent

What this direction rules out

  • Stock photography of clinicians or residents
  • Screenshots of the legacy interface
  • Invented UI the product doesn't have
  • An elaborate "connected platform" diagram
  • Navy, teal, or anything else in the category's default blue
  • Gradients, glows, glassmorphism, abstract blobs
  • Isometric artwork and decorative iconography
  • A "we're refreshing our website" banner

On that last one: a finished small site shouldn't announce that it's temporary. The thinness is only a liability if we point at it. If the client insists, it belongs in the footer as housekeeping — not a top bar, and never in the hero.
