One system for the work behind your workforce.

Time & Attendance

Time and Attendance is the primary module of the SBV software. Employee information and configuration are maintained here and used by the other modules.

Every addition, edit or deletion can be attributed to a user, date and time.

Scheduling & Accruals

The Scheduler provides a budget-controlled scheduling process. Staffing levels can adjust based on census, and the Accruals module calculates and validates accrual pay types.

Changes made by one user immediately appear to all users.

Payroll Based Journal

The Payroll Based Journal module creates the XML file required by CMS for electronic submission of direct-care hours. It handles PBJ job types, overnight shifts and hours that pass through midnight.

Each XML file can contain one day to three months of data.

Licenses, Health & In-Service

Maintain primary and secondary licenses and certifications, track physical, PPD and MMR dates, and report on mandatory and optional in-services.

Reports identify missing requirements and upcoming due dates.

Payroll Interfaces

SBV creates the payroll file in the format specified by your payroll company. Your payroll system imports and validates the file, and total hours are compared between both systems.

A test file is processed with your payroll provider before go-live.

Reporting

Over 250 reports are available in PDF or Excel, including employee, time card, punch, hours, scheduling, staffing, accrual and attendance reports.

Custom reporting, Excel templates and raw-data export are also available.

Staffing a facility is not the same as staffing an office.

SBV software is designed to handle the rules and staffing requirements of long-term care facilities. Multiple shifts, labor levels, pay rules, PBJ job titles, employee licenses and health requirements can all be maintained in the same system.

PBJ hours can be allocated by department or employee. The system handles overnight shifts and hours that pass through midnight.
