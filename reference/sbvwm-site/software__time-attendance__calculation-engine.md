# Calculation Engine | SBV Workforce Management

**Source:** https://www.sbvwm.com/software/time-attendance/calculation-engine/

---

* All standard calculation features like rounding, grace and revisions
  * Daily, weekly, bi-weekly, consecutive and custom overtime rules supported
  * Premiums – shift differential, time zone differential, guarantees, maximum with multiple qualifiers
  * Highly configurable break and lunch calculation available – paid breaks, cumulative lunches, etc.
  * Holidays – multiple qualifiers and calculation parameters that can use information from the employee, time card and schedule to calculate the correct holiday and worked holiday hours
