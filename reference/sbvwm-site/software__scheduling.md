# Scheduling | SBV Workforce Management

**Source:** https://www.sbvwm.com/software/scheduling/

---

The Scheduler module provides a budgetary controlled scheduling process. Employees cancelling or changing schedules leave a placeholder to quickly identify schedule changes or planned absences. Placeholders can be promptly approved with a single click, by your payroll department, to create a payment of hours.

### Features

  * Multi-user – changes made by one user immediately appear to all users
  * Budget controlled schedules enforce compliance with your staffing levels
  * Staffing levels automatically adjust based on census
  * Detailed history of schedule transfers is maintained
  * Planned absences can be entered before building schedules
  * Available schedules show your staffing needs
  * Scheduled based restrictions at time clock
