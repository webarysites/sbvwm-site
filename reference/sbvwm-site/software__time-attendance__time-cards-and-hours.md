# Time Cards and Hours | SBV Workforce Management

**Source:** https://www.sbvwm.com/software/time-attendance/time-cards-and-hours/

---

* Support punch and/or hours entry
  * Assign labor levels at punch or schedule level
  * Departmental changes can be entered at time clock
  * Hours can be added or deducted
  * Salaried employees can have auto punches or auto hours
