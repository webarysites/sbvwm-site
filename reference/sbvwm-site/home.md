## Main content

SBV develops industry-specific workforce management software solutions. Our **primary vertical market** is managed long term care facilities. Our software solutions are sold and supported directly by SBV. We provide **installation** , **setup** and **training** as well as software customization, support and maintenance contracts. We offer a choice of biometric time clocks solutions, and interface with most major payroll software and services.
