# Health Tracking | SBV Workforce Management

**Source:** https://www.sbvwm.com/software/health-tracking/

---

This module tracks the employee physical, PPD, and MMR (measles, mumps and rubella) data. If employees are PPD Positive, then CXR and TB Screen dates are required.

  * Tracks both the last received and the next or upcoming due dates
  * The management reports indicate both missing and due dates
  * Employee form letters can be generated and included with their pay check or schedule, advising the employee of upcoming expiration dates
